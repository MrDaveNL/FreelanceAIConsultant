## What are AI solutions?

An AI solution is an application in which artificial intelligence solves a concrete problem in your work: pulling information out of documents, answering questions based on internal knowledge, sorting requests, or making predictions. The word "solution" matters here. An AI solution isn't just the software; it's the whole of the model, the data, the integrations, the working process, and the people who use it.

That distinction explains why many AI projects succeed technically and still deliver little in practice. The model works, but nobody has thought through who checks the outcome, what happens when it's unsure, or how it fits into the daily routine.

I'm an [independent AI consultant](/about) and I don't build AI solutions myself. I help you decide which solution makes sense, help choose a solution or build partner with you, and test from your side whether the result works. The building itself is done by your own team or a partner you contract directly.

## Examples of AI solutions

Most AI solutions that are actually used inside organisations fall into a handful of categories:

- **Document processing.** Pulling data from invoices, contracts, applications or case files and preparing it for review.
- **Knowledge assistants.** Staff find answers in policies, handbooks and past cases without searching themselves.
- **Classifying and routing.** Automatically sorting incoming emails, tickets or reports and routing them to the right person.
- **Summarising and drafting.** Preparing reports, quotes or write-ups based on existing information.
- **Forecasting.** Estimating demand, churn or risk based on historical data.
- **AI agents.** Systems that carry out steps in your software themselves, within agreed limits.

## Custom AI solutions versus standard

Not every question calls for custom AI solutions. Often a well-configured standard solution is the fastest and cheapest route. A few rules of thumb:

- **Choose standard** when several vendors offer the same thing, the task isn't unique to your organisation, and you mainly want value fast.
- **Choose custom** when the solution touches on why customers choose you, your data or way of working is distinctive, or no standard package gives you the control you need.
- **Choose a combination** in most other cases: standard building blocks, with a custom-built layer for your rules, data and integrations.

How you assess standard software and what integration involves is covered on the page about [choosing, integrating or building AI software](/ai-development/ai-software).

## AI solutions for small and medium-sized businesses

The same principles apply for smaller businesses, but with less room for experiments. A failed project weighs more heavily in a smaller organisation. Three pieces of advice:

- **Start with the software you already have.** Many packages include AI features that, with the right setup, cover a good part of the need without custom work.
- **Pick one bottleneck with a clear owner.** One process done well beats five done half well.
- **Only have something custom built once standard demonstrably falls short.** Custom work brings ongoing support costs, and you need to be able to carry those.
