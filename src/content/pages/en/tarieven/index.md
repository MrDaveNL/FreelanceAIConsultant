## What does AI consulting cost?

There is no standard price for AI consulting, because the questions vary so much. A leadership team that wants a shared view in one afternoon needs something different from an organization looking for an eighteen-month roadmap. What I can do is translate the most common questions into fixed packages, each with a clear price or range.

### What moves the price

Within a package, the amount moves with a few concrete factors:

- **Scope:** a single department or process domain is reviewed faster than the whole organization.
- **Number of people involved:** more business units, interviews or participants means more preparation and sessions.
- **Number of vendors or options:** in a selection process, the number of parties to assess sets the size of the work.
- **Time per month:** for an ongoing role, the number of fixed days per month sets the monthly fee.

All amounts on this page are excluding VAT and travel expenses.

## Why no hourly rate?

An hourly rate rewards the wrong things. It makes more hours more attractive than a faster outcome, and it shifts the conversation to the timesheet instead of the decision you want to make. With a fixed price or an agreed range set in advance, you know where you stand before the start, and the collaboration is about the result.

Scoped advisory work, workshops and second opinions also get a fixed price, with no minimum engagement.

## Freelance consultant versus agency: the cost difference

An agency or large consultancy has an organization built around the consultant, such as account management and often a mix of senior and junior consultants, and those costs sit in the rate. With me, you always work with the senior you spoke to; there are no juniors on your project. The trade-off is that a one-person practice has less delivery capacity; for that I suggest partners you contract directly, with no margin for me.

Which form fits best depends on your question, not only on the price.
