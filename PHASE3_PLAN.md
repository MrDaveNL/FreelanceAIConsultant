## Summary

**58 translate, 2 localize, 6 skip** (of 66 total non-live content/article pages). The 2 localize
cases both involve Dutch government/statistics content (CBS adoption stats, MIT/SLIM-mkb subsidy
schemes) inside the `ai-voor-bedrijven` cluster. 5 of the 6 skips are `markten` pages already
`noindex` in NL — not a translation decision so much as "not ready in either language yet." The
6th skip (AI specialist salary) is NL-market compensation data that would mislead if translated
without real EN-market figures. Two keyword-collision risks are pre-empted by design (AI
consultancy vs. services index, AI strategy vs. AI Strategy Sprint) and one is a soft watch-item
(the freelance/independent-AI-consultant comparison article) rather than a true conflict.
